The problem

Work shouldn't own your evenings.

When your workplace lives inside your browser, boundaries blur. Late-night calendar invites arrive, rest gets delayed, and daily quiet time disappears under constant notifications. You don't need another calendar planner to squeeze in more tasks. You need a Personal Time Firewall.

How it works

Three steps to a protected schedule.

1

Define your boundaries

Set your work hours, lunch breaks, and Sabbath rest days once. Sabbath monitors your schedule in the background.

2

Automate boundary enforcement

When an off-hour invite arrives, Sabbath shields your schedule. Choose Automatic Mode to decline off-hour meetings instantly and send three alternative workday slots, or Smart Mode to approve exceptions with one click.

3

Reclaim your quiet time

Opening a browser tab during off-hours unlocks your New Tab Sanctuary — featuring peaceful scripture, reflection prompts, guided S.O.A.P. journaling, and fasting tracking.
